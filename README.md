# Karen's Notes

A notes app that lives in your notification bar.

**Designed by Karen Lee** — to her exact spec, in her words:

> "In my notification bar cuz I need to be able to access it quick. I don't want to have to load the app. I would love to have it in the notification bar so I just click on it, add my note."

## What it does

- **One quiet notification, always in the bar: "🎤 Add a note."** Tap it and the app is already listening. Say the note; it writes itself down. No loading the app, no hunting through screens.
- **Voice first.** The note apps Karen found kept throwing a keyboard at her — "a keyboard that I don't even use." Here the keyboard appears in exactly one place: when she chooses to type.
- **Big print.** Notes list and reading view are set large, because that's how she reads.
- **A picture when you want one.** Attach a photo to a note from the camera or gallery.
- **Only the stuff you actually need.** No account, no sign-in, no cloud, no ads. Notes live on the phone, in app-private storage, and nowhere else. The only permission the app asks for is the microphone — it can't listen, it can't take the note.

## Why it exists

Karen had a notes tool she loved once, riding in her notification bar, and lost it. Everything since has been slower, smaller, and keyboard-first. This is that lost app, rebuilt the way she asked for it — and nothing else added.
